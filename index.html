    About this map

    The Yei pilot shows significant change in building presence between two years. Presence is mean-pooled to 4 m, stored as integer percentage, spatially matched within 4 m, then differenced. Only changes of at least 30 percentage points are retained and styled in your browser. Change is aggregated as the map zooms out and resolves to sparse 4 m loss/gain pixels when zoomed in. Stable buildings appear as a quiet grey underlay; empty pixels are transparent. It does not estimate a building count. Whether a change reflects displacement, return, flooding, or ordinary growth is the interpretation carried by the labelled places, not by the colour alone.

    Two periods are shown, split at the 2018 revitalised peace agreement: a civil-war period (2016→2018) and a post-agreement period (2019→2023).
