About this map

The unit shown is the change in building counts between two years, per 1.2 km cell. Settlement in South Sudan is dominated by tukuls, so building counts are used as a measurable proxy for where people live — but the map itself only claims buildings gained or lost. Whether a change reflects displacement, return, flooding, or ordinary growth is the interpretation carried by the labelled places, not by the colour alone.

Two periods are shown, split at the 2018 revitalised peace agreement: a civil-war period (2016→2018) and a post-agreement period (2019→2023).
